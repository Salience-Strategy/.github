## Salience Strategy

Salience Strategy is a search and AI visibility agency for B2B companies that sell through a sales call. It publishes the pages and third-party mentions that get a company named when a buyer asks Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity who to talk to, and it reports the sales calls booked from those answers. $2,000 a month, no contract.

[`salience-baseline`](https://github.com/Salience-Strategy/salience-baseline) holds the script we run before the work starts: it asks a company's buyer questions in four AI engines and Google and counts the answers that name the company (MIT).
